Application Name: Student Hub

Short Description:
A modern university management application that enables students to manage attendance, academic records, campus exploration, announcements and other academic services.

Full Description:
Student Hub is an Android application developed to simplify university life by providing students with one centralized platform for managing academic activities.

Key Features:
• Secure Login and Registration
• Student Records Management
• Attendance Tracking
• Attendance Reports
• Course Registration
• Campus Explorer
• Camera Integration
• GPS Location Services
• Announcements
• SQLite Database
• Professional Dashboard
• Material Design User Interface

Student Hub improves communication, organization and access to important academic information while providing a secure and user-friendly experience.

Category: Education
Target Users: University Students
Current Version: 1.0.0
Minimum Android: Android 8.0 (API 26)
Developer: Celestine Nguhi
University: Mount Kenya University
