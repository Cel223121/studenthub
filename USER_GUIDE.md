# Student Hub Pro - User Guide

Welcome to **Student Hub Pro**, your all-in-one university management companion. This guide will help you navigate and use the essential features of the application.

## 1. Getting Started
*   **Splash Screen**: Upon opening the app, you will see a professional animated splash screen while the system initializes.
*   **Login & Registration**: Secure your account using your student email. Passwords are encrypted for your safety.
*   **Auto-Login**: Once logged in, the app remembers your session. You won't need to log in again unless you manually sign out.

## 2. Professional Dashboard
The dashboard is your central hub for all services:
*   **Live Status**: Check the real-time "System Status" and "Total Registered Students" at a glance.
*   **Module Navigation**: Use the clean grid layout to jump to Profile, Attendance, Records, or News.
*   **Smart Search**: Use the search bar at the top of the dashboard to quickly filter and find any module you need.

## 3. Student Records (CRUD)
*   **Add Student**: Enroll new students with full details (Name, Email, Course, Phone).
*   **Academic Records**: View a professional list of all students.
*   **Search & Filter**: Find specific records instantly by typing a name or course.
*   **Edit/Delete**: Long-press a student card or use the action buttons to update or remove records.

## 4. Attendance Module
*   **Take Attendance**: Search for students by name and mark them as "Present" or "Absent".
*   **Attendance Reports**: View a detailed log of every attendance record, including student names and timestamps.

## 5. E-Learning Center
*   **Lecture Notes**: Access direct links to official documentation for Java, Android, and SQL.
*   **Assignments**: Link directly to your university's submission portal.
*   **Learning Videos**: Watch curated high-quality tutorials to boost your academic performance.

## 6. Live Portal (Networking)
*   Interact with the "Live Portal" to see a simulated real-time list of students currently online in the system.

## 7. Profile & Settings
*   **Profile**: View your full academic identity, including year of study and contact information.
*   **Dark Mode**: Switch between Light and Dark themes via the Settings menu.
*   **Security**: Change your password at any time through the secure Settings dialog.
